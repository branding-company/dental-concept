Name

Three names, one recommendation

Recommended
ayg
ԱյգАйг

Armenian for dawn. Three letters, easy to say in all three languages, and it already points at soft morning light, which becomes the visual idea. Pairs with “Dental Atelier” in English.

luys
ԼույսЛуйс

Armenian for light. Meaning is very clear to locals, but Latin spelling may be read as “loys” by visitors, which weakens it for tourism.

sirun
ՍիրունСирун

Armenian for lovely. Warm and instantly understood by Armenians and diaspora, but also a common first name, so it feels less like a brand.

I could not check trademarks or existing businesses with these names. That needs a real search before any of them is used outside a concept.

Wordmark and tagline

The arch is the one shape

A window of morning light. The mark is an arch with a quiet smile line inside. The same arch shapes photos, buttons and doctor portraits, so the site feels like one calm room.

Colour and type

Warm white, one mineral green

Soft and light, no clinical blue. The green is deep enough for text and buttons to pass contrast. Dark mode is an evening version of the same room.

Take your time.

Не нужно спешить.

Կարող եք չշտապել։

Onest at light weights carries English and Russian. Noto Sans Armenian on the same weight axis carries Armenian, so all three scripts look like one family and can all “breathe” the same way. The risk I am taking: very light, very large type, and a weight that slowly rises and falls. Small text stays at regular weight for contrast.

Structure

One slow scroll, one goal: a booked consultation

  1. OpeningThe hook, one action: see your new smile.
  2. Smile PreviewSelfie or sample face, on-device landmarks, whitening, veneers, alignment slider. Send the result with a request.
  3. Treatments, explainedImplants, veneers, aligners, whitening, crowns, kids. A 3D or animated step-by-step each, with “does it hurt” answered.
  4. Cost plannerPick a goal or treatments. AMD range, visits, timeline, monthly instalments. Sample data, marked.
  5. Before and afterSliders filterable by treatment. Sample cases, marked.
  6. Technology3D scanner, CBCT, microscope, smile design, as calm close-ups.
  7. DoctorsPortraits in the arch, story, languages, book with this doctor.
  8. Your first visitStep by step, sedation options, kids’ corner. The fear-free section.
  9. Treatment in YerevanUSD, EUR, RUB prices, sample comparison abroad, trip planner, send X-rays for a plan.
  10. BookingService, doctor, slot. WhatsApp, Telegram, Viber. Emergency button.
  11. Reviews and FAQMarked as sample. Dentist and MedicalClinic structured data.
