## What the best do
- Schüco: homeowner configurator (colour, face width, security, sound, thermal standard, shading, ventilation); ends by passing the lead to a local partner. Fabricator-side Product Configurator runs a technical check (SchüCal) at every step and can produce a quote instantly.
- Reynaers: heavy on professional tools (Docu Portal ~20,000 catalogue pages, hardware configurator, access-control configurator).
- VEKA: WinDoPlan online planning for partners, incl. wind-load calculation; Digital Building Solutions group.
- Internorm: design styles (studio, home pure, home soft, ambiente), door designer, renovation / window replacement guide, "made in Austria", distribution-partner search, Mix & Match.
- Pattern: configurator + separate audience paths + downloads + partner finder. All are global brands with huge budgets; none shows a local factory floor.

## Armenian market
- Spyur lists 22 organisations under metal-plastic windows (RU category) and many more in HY; directory-style listings, not branded experiences.
- Public tenders (armeps) specify windows by profile thickness (60-62 mm), glass unit (4+4), opening types, m² totals: bulk buyers think in specs and quantities.
- No keyword-volume data was available from these searches. Needs Search Console / Keyword Planner before claiming specific demand.

## Gaps in Armenia (my reading)
1. No online configurator with real thermal / sound values and AMD estimate.
2. No proof of manufacturing (production process, certificates).
3. No real project gallery with before/after.
4. No clear paths and downloads for architects / developers / dealers.
5. Quote and measurement booking is phone-first.

## How EURO.G stands out
Local manufacturer that can show the factory, give an honest estimate in AMD, and turn it into a qualified lead with a booked measurement visit. Signature: rotatable 3D profile, exploded view, thermal view.
