Project: CHOCOLATE BONBONS ATELIER — CONCEPT (fictional brand, label "Concept").
A premium Armenian bonbon atelier: hand-painted bonbons with Armenian
flavours, gift boxes, seasonal collections, corporate gifts.

Read playbook/CLAUDE.md and playbook/style-log.md first and follow them.

GOAL
A portfolio piece that shows our full capabilities, gives a real wow effect
and proves business value: a site that would make a chocolate brand sell
more boxes, bigger boxes and corporate orders. It must look and work NOTHING
like our earlier showcases:
- Ararat Heights: dark, cinematic, classical serif, real estate
- EURO.G: bright technical grid, drawings, grotesk + mono, engineering
- Kristelle des Chocolats (a real chocolate client already in our portfolio):
  do not reuse its look; this one must clearly feel like a different brand.

BRAND (propose, I approve)
- 3 name options for the atelier (short, works in HY/RU/EN, memorable), a
  logo/wordmark, a tagline in each language.
- Personality: sensual, playful, generous, couture: a modern chocolatier,
  not an old-fashioned confectioner.
- Collection of ~16 bonbons, each with a name, colour, filling and story.
  Mix Armenian character with classics, for example: apricot and thyme,
  pomegranate ganache, mulberry, walnut "sujukh" praline, gata caramel,
  Armenian brandy truffle, tarragon and lime, rose petal jam, sea-buckthorn,
  salted caramel, hazelnut gianduja, coffee with cardamom, dark 70% single
  origin.

STEP 1: RESEARCH FIRST (short report before designing)
Study the best chocolate and patisserie sites (e.g. Pierre Marcolini,
Läderach, Jacques Genin, Patrick Roger, Compartés, Vosges, Dandelion, plus
award-winning food sites on Awwwards) and the Armenian market (Grand Candy,
local boutiques, how people here buy gift chocolate: holidays, March 8,
New Year, weddings, corporate gifts, diaspora sending gifts home). Tell me:
what the best do, what is missing in Armenia, and how this brand stands out.

DESIGN DIRECTION: "EDIBLE COUTURE" (refine after research)
- Colour: rich, saturated, appetising. Deep cocoa and cream as the base,
  with each bonbon's own jewel colour taking over sections as you scroll
  (apricot orange, pomegranate red, pistachio green, rose, gold dust).
- Type: an expressive, characterful display face with a sensual italic and a
  soft, warm body font. Do not use fonts already used (Forum, Manrope,
  IBM Plex, Archivo).
- Layout: editorial and playful: asymmetric compositions, oversized macro
  product shots, bonbons breaking out of the grid, horizontal-scroll
  collection, sticky colour transitions. No technical grids, no dark
  cinematic bands.
- Imagery: one consistent campaign style: macro studio photography,
  glossy shells, cut-open bonbons showing fillings, cocoa dust, hands
  painting, warm soft light. AI-generated (Canva) where needed, plus 3D or
  WebGL bonbons rendered in code.

FIRST 5 SECONDS (the hook)
A glossy bonbon (or molten chocolate surface) that reacts to the cursor or
finger in real time: light glides over the shell, chocolate ripples where
you touch. Then it cracks open and the filling colour floods the screen into
the next section.

SIGNATURE WOW MOMENT: BUILD YOUR BOX
The visitor fills a real-looking gift box: bonbons are dragged or tapped in
and drop with satisfying physics, the box size changes (4/9/16/25), the
price updates in AMD, a ribbon colour is chosen, the lid closes and ties
itself, and a handwritten-style gift card is typed. Saved boxes can be
shared by link ("I made this box for you"). Must feel just as good on a
phone (tap to add, haptic-style micro animations).

FEATURES (each must help the business sell)
- Collection: every bonbon has its page or card: cut-open view, filling,
  tasting notes, ingredients, allergens, best before, pairing.
- Flavour finder: a short playful quiz (fruity, nutty, boozy, floral,
  intense?) that suggests a ready box, with "add to box".
- Gift delivery in Yerevan: date and time slot, recipient, card message,
  surprise delivery option; "Send a gift to Armenia" for the diaspora with
  prices also shown in USD/EUR/RUB.
- Seasonal and occasion collections: New Year, Valentine's Day, March 8,
  weddings and christenings (favours), birthdays.
- Corporate gifts: logo printed on the box or on bonbons, quantity price
  tiers, live mock-up of their logo on the lid, quote request form.
- Atelier story: scroll-told making process (tempering, painting shells,
  filling, sealing) with real-time motion.
- Tasting workshops: booking with a calendar.
- Pairing guide: bonbons with Armenian brandy, wine, coffee, tea.
- Cart and checkout demo (clearly says no real payment), AMD prices.
- HY / RU / EN written natively (Armenian must sound native, never
  translated); mobile-first; fast; SEO, Product structured data, FAQ.

WOW STANDARD
Amaze within 5 seconds and keep surprising while scrolling. Award-level
quality (Awwwards "Site of the Day"), not a template or a generic AI site.
It should make people hungry. Smooth micro-interactions on every control,
and the wow must survive on phones. Before showing me anything, critique it
honestly as a senior creative director ("what's generic here? does it look
like Kristelle or our other showcases?") and fix it first.

PROCESS
1) Research report  2) Brand (names, logo, palette, type), structure, and
2–3 directions for the opening as live previews → I approve  3) Build the
live demo  4) Walkthrough video, screenshots of every feature (desktop +
phone), case study  5) Add to both decks and the portfolio page. After
approval, add the new style to playbook/style-log.md.

TECH
Custom code (Next.js/React; WebGL/three.js for the bonbon and physics for
the box). GitHub repo branding-company/bonbons-concept (I create it empty
