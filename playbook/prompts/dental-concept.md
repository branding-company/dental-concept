Project: DENTAL CLINIC — CONCEPT (fictional brand, label "Concept").
A premium modern dental clinic in Yerevan: aesthetic dentistry (veneers,
whitening, aligners), implants, full-mouth restoration, family and kids
care, and dental tourism for the diaspora and visitors from abroad.

The showcase playbook is in playbook/ in this repo. Read playbook/CLAUDE.md
and playbook/style-log.md first and follow them.

GOAL
A portfolio piece that shows our full capabilities, gives a real wow effect
and proves business value: a site that turns nervous visitors into booked
consultations and brings in high-value treatments (implants, veneers) and
patients from abroad. It must look and work NOTHING like our earlier
showcases:
- Ararat Heights: dark, cinematic, classical serif, real estate
- EURO.G: bright technical grid, drawings, grotesk + mono, engineering
- Chocolate bonbons atelier: saturated jewel colours, sensual, playful
  (check style-log.md for its final look)
Also avoid the dental cliché: cold blue-and-white, stock smiles, tooth icons.

BRAND (propose, I approve)
- 3 name options (short, works in HY/RU/EN), a logo/wordmark, a tagline in
  each language.
- Personality: calm, warm, precise, reassuring: closer to a boutique hotel or
  a design studio than a hospital. "You're in good hands" without saying it.
- Team of 5–6 doctors (sample), each with a specialty, photo, short story.

STEP 1: RESEARCH FIRST (short report before designing)
Study the best dental and aesthetic clinic sites worldwide (e.g. Kois
Center, London Smile Clinic, Dentalclinic Zürich, Straumann and Invisalign
patient pages, award-winning healthcare sites on Awwwards) and the Armenian
market (leading Yerevan clinics, dental tourism to Armenia from Russia,
Europe, the US and the Middle East, how patients choose: price, trust,
before/after, doctors, technology). Tell me: what the best do, what is
missing in Armenia, and how this clinic stands out.

DESIGN DIRECTION: "QUIET CONFIDENCE" (refine after research)
- Colour: soft, light and warm: warm white, porcelain, pale sand, one calm
  natural accent (e.g. sage, soft clay or mineral green). No clinical blue.
- Type: a refined, humane pairing with lots of air; light weights, generous
  spacing. Do not reuse fonts already in the style log.
- Layout: calm and spacious, slow rhythm, large soft-light photography,
  rounded gentle shapes, content revealed gently. Feels unhurried.
- Imagery: one consistent campaign style: natural daylight, real-looking
  people (not stock grins), calm treatment rooms, hands, materials,
  technology close-ups. AI-generated (Canva) in one look; 3D in code where
  it explains something.
- Motion: slow, breathing, soothing; nothing jumpy. The site itself should
  lower anxiety.

FIRST 5 SECONDS (the hook)
A calm, beautiful scene that "breathes" (soft light moving across a face or
a room) with one line that speaks to the visitor's real feeling (fear,
embarrassment about their smile) and a single clear action: "See your new
smile".

SIGNATURE WOW MOMENT: SMILE PREVIEW
The visitor takes a selfie or uploads a photo; the site finds the smile in
the browser (face landmarks, nothing uploaded to a server) and shows a live
before/after slider: whitening shades, veneers, aligned teeth. They can
compare options and send the result with a consultation request. Clearly
labelled as an illustrative simulation, not a medical result. Include a set
of sample faces for people who don't want to use their own photo. Must work
smoothly on phones (front camera).

FEATURES (each must help the clinic win patients)
- Treatment cost planner: choose treatments or a goal ("replace a missing
  tooth", "a whiter, even smile"), see a price range in AMD, number of
  visits, timeline, and monthly instalment options.
- Dental tourism: "Treatment in Yerevan" path with prices also in
  USD/EUR/RUB, a comparison with typical prices abroad (sample), a trip
  planner (visits per stay, days needed, airport pick-up, partner hotels),
  and an online pre-consultation: send photos or X-rays, get a plan.
- Before/after gallery with sliders, filterable by treatment (sample cases).
- Explained treatments: implants, veneers, aligners, whitening, crowns,
  kids care; each with an interactive 3D or animated explanation of what
  actually happens, how long, does it hurt.
- Technology section: 3D scanner, CBCT, microscope, digital smile design;
  shown as calm close-up motion, explained simply.
- Doctors: profiles, specialties, languages spoken, booking with that
  doctor.
- Booking: choose service, doctor, date and time slot; confirmation;
  WhatsApp / Telegram / Viber contact; emergency appointment button.
- Comfort and fear-free care: what a first visit looks like step by step,
  sedation options, kids' corner.
- Reviews and FAQ (marked as sample), Dentist and MedicalClinic structured
  data, local SEO.
- HY / RU / EN written natively (Armenian must sound native, never
  translated); mobile-first; fast; accessible (good contrast, large tap
  targets).
- Medical content stays accurate and careful: no guaranteed results, no
  invented certifications; sample data clearly marked.

WOW STANDARD
Amaze within 5 seconds and keep surprising while scrolling. Award-level
quality (Awwwards "Site of the Day"), not a template or a generic AI site.
Here the wow is calm and refined, not loud: people should feel relaxed and
impressed at the same time. Smooth micro-interactions on every control, and
the wow must survive on phones. Before showing me anything, critique it
honestly as a senior creative director ("what's generic here? does it look
like any dental template or our other showcases?") and fix it first.

PROCESS
1) Research report  2) Brand (names, logo, palette, type), structure, and
2–3 directions for the opening as live previews → I approve  3) Build the
live demo  4) Walkthrough video, screenshots of every feature (desktop +
phone), case study  5) Add to both decks and the portfolio page. After
approval, add the new style to playbook/style-log.md.

TECH
Custom code (Next.js/React; in-browser face landmarks, e.g. MediaPipe, for
the smile preview; three.js for treatment explanations). GitHub repo
branding-company/dental-concept. Deploy to a live demo URL.
